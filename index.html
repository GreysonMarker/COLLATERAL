Controls & abilities

Phone
Left stick to move. Drag anywhere else to look. The camera gently follows your travel direction when you stop dragging. Tap JUMP once while airborne to start flight, and once while flying to stop. On the ground, hold JUMP to charge and release to jump higher (full charge at 1.5 seconds). In the air, hold JUMP to rise; release to hover. While flying, push the stick forward and aim the camera up or down to climb or dive. On foot, tap the speed button to cycle WALK, SPRINT, and SLOW WALK. Whenever airborne (jumping, falling, or flying), tap the speed button to cycle NORMAL, FAST, and SLOW for flight. Your selected ground speed returns on landing. SLOW keeps an upright hovering pose.

Defense
Hold BLOCK / F to guard. Tap PARRY / Q for a slap-back counter: the yellow outline and shield mark its 13/30-second active window. Every attack type can be redirected. When yellow ends, the red spark marks 0.5 seconds of vulnerability. Parry recharges in 2.5 seconds. Quick punches stun unguarded heroes for 2.2 seconds without knockback; blocking negates them. Normal punches tumble an unguarded hero or slide a guarding hero backward. Serious punches launch even a guarding hero. Hero-hit shockwaves are visual only.

Super strength
QUICK (J on Computer): rapid close hit; interrupts your other punches. NORMAL (K on Computer): a heavier close hit that can collapse a building. SERIOUS (L on Computer): wind up, then release a broad forward blast across 450 meters. Aim with the camera throughout the punch, including down at the ground. Quick and Normal reach a few meters; Serious projects a long shockwave. Buttons show recovery time.

Grab and throw
Tap GRAB, press E in Computer mode, or G in Mobile mode while facing a nearby player, practice dummy, civilian, or car. While flying, grabs on players and the dummy reach normal-punch distance; ground grabs and civilian/car grabs keep their usual range. A blue flash signals the short windup. Aim with the camera and tap again to throw; holding for three seconds throws automatically. Misses have a short recovery. Walking and flight remain available while holding. Throws aim through the heat-vision dot and damage buildings or streets on impact. Thrown civilians die on impact. Cars explode into fire on hard ground landings; building impacts do not ignite them.

Combat opponent
In single-player, open Pause and turn Combat ON. Your selected training character pursues, blocks, trades punches, and fires heat vision. Turn it OFF to return to a stationary dummy. Both fighters start with full health when combat is toggled.

Battle damage
Every 100 health lost adds a stage of damage, from torn fabric to bruises, cuts, blood, and shredded capes. All six characters recover their clean appearance when health resets.

Health
1,000 health. Quick 25 · Normal 50 · Serious 100 · Flight ram 75 · Head-on collision 150 each. Held building impacts deal 15; direct ground slams deal 60. Slides deal up to 10 per second, capped at 30 per grab. Punch/throw collision damage is half. Knockouts respawn after 3 seconds.

Heat vision
Hold HEAT, R in Computer mode, or E in Mobile mode to fire. In combat, deal 25 damage per second with a 3-second firing budget and 10-second recharge. Releasing the button preserves remaining charge. Aim with the camera. Twin beams pierce standing buildings with focused tunnels, with almost no knockback. Cars retain narrow beam cuts; pedestrians collapse into limp ragdolls. Aim through the tiny dot above the hero. Range: 118.125 meters. Sweeping across structural supports can bring down the floors above. Cuts stay until you reset the city.

Destruction
Normal flight punches local openings. Fast direct hits can collapse towers; high hits blast away roughly the upper fifth. Grazes strip the side and only collapse a tower if enough supports are lost. Collapsing sections tumble and clear away. Building damage stays until you reset the city. Returning downtown does not repair it.

Ground impacts
Hard downward hits excavate craters. Fast ground slides tear trenches through the road and its markings. Nearby pedestrians and cars are knocked away; traffic brakes for deep holes. Charged jumps leave a tiny takeoff scuff. Starting fast flight releases a damaging shockwave. Ground scars stay for this session.

Full-screen on iPhone

Computer controls
J / K / L: Quick / Normal / Serious punch · E: Grab / Throw · Hold R: Heat vision · Hold F: Block · Q: Parry · Tab: Lock on.
Hold Shift for Fast flight or sprinting. Release to return to your selected cruise speed or walking. Tap Shift to switch Normal / Slow flight. Key labels remain on the action buttons.
Mobile keeps its touch controls and speed button.

Head-on collision
Two heroes in controlled flight colliding at a combined closing speed of at least 1.5× FAST flight speed (213.75 m/s) trigger a massive circular shockwave with a 253.125-meter radius. Both heroes are launched backward with Serious Punch knockback; surrounding buildings, roads, vehicles, trees, and pedestrians take heavy damage. Angled charges qualify too. Both heroes must be flying toward the collision; thrown, stunned, held, and falling heroes cannot trigger it. Slower hits and chases retain normal tackle rules.

Midair recovery
After a punch or throw launches you, two uninterrupted seconds in the air unlock your controls. Tap RECOVER / Space to resume flight. Another hit or a collision restarts the timer. Landing follows normal ground recovery, and you cannot recover while held.

Momentum
Punch launches and throws add your movement at the instant of impact or release, including forward, sideways, upward, and downward speed. A Serious Punch wave keeps the momentum it had when fired. Flight tackles carry your existing speed without adding it twice. Quick punches against heroes still stun without launching.

Aerial grapples
While moving in flight, grabbed heroes hang below your grip and struggle. Fly into an airborne hero at full FAST speed to shoulder-tackle them for up to two seconds. Keep steering to drive them ahead of you. Stop moving or leave flight to release them forward, or dive into the ground to slam them. Blocks and parries stop a tackle. After one successful tackle, throw five punches to recharge it. Cancelled windups and automatic flight collisions do not count. Until recharged, another full-speed collision deals a Normal Punch instead. The grab button displays how many punches remain.

Combat lock-on
Tap LOCK, Tab in Computer mode, or L in Mobile mode to track the player or practice dummy closest to your aiming dot. Tap again to unlock. The amber dot stays the same size. While holding a target, look freely to aim your throw. Lock stays selected and camera tracking resumes when you release them. Lock has no distance limit. On Computer, hold the primary mouse or trackpad click to temporarily look and aim away without losing the selected target. Release to resume camera tracking. Your stationary training partner uses a selectable hero and the same ragdoll and collision systems, in single-player only.
